# Soclo plan-my-week

Read the current /plan-my-week playbook and references/soclo-workflow.md first. Read soclo_brand_get, soclo_library_search and soclo_destinations_list. Use only saved facts and owned assets, with a real date range and timezone. Include audience, message, format, destination and the owner's desired cadence; flag essential missing information.

Draft the week in this host. If the owner asks to save or continue a durable draft, use soclo_work_drafts_discover and soclo_work_drafts_read, then soclo_work_drafts_edit with the exact base last shown, slot, original stable clientRequestId and new draft. Resolve conflicts without silently refreshing the base or overwriting another client's work.

For a requested advert storyboard use soclo_host_plan_prepare, its complete guide packet and an exact import; for prices use the ready-plan quote flow. A weekly plan or saved draft starts no generation and enables no Autopilot schedule. Any later generation, publication or Autopilot setting change must use that tool's separate quote/preview and owner approval; do not silently schedule or spend.
