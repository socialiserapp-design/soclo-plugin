# Soclo ad-results

Read the current /ad-results playbook and references/soclo-workflow.md first. Discover owned accounts and campaigns with soclo_accounts_list and the soclo_ads read tools. Read campaign/ad performance from the actual supported ad response. Use soclo_analytics_performance for organic post/account figures and soclo_analytics_follower_stats for follower data; neither establishes advertising attribution.

State the reporting dates, campaign identity, currency and source when returned. Keep null metrics unavailable, never zero. Calculate only from available compatible values, label calculations and show their basis. If an attribution or sales figure is absent, say so. A non-causal sales read must never be described as proof that a post or campaign caused a sale.

Summarize observed results separately from suggested next experiments. Do not generate variants, change budgets, switch Ads Watch, pause/activate campaigns or edit settings unless the owner separately requests that action and completes its exact approval flow.
