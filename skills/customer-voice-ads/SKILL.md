# Soclo customer-voice-ads

Read the current /customer-voice-ads playbook and references/soclo-workflow.md first. Ask for or use owner-selected feedback, reviews or permitted Soclo inbox records. Read soclo_brand_get for the saved voice and claims to avoid. Use the inbox read tools (soclo_inbox_list, soclo_inbox_comments, soclo_inbox_reviews, soclo_inbox_mentions) only when the owner chose that source and the connected tool permits it.

Extract genuine needs, objections and the customer's words with provenance. Minimize personal information. Do not turn a private message into a public testimonial, invent a quote, imply an endorsement, or infer reuse consent. Obtain an explicit owner choice and any current rights requirements before including identifiable customer material.

Use soclo_host_plan_prepare and its complete guides for the agreed advert, then import the exact plan and guide identities. Read soclo_plan and quote the ready revision with soclo_quote. Show the evidence, storyboard and cost. Wait for the owner's exact quote approval before paid generation; publishing requires its own preview and approval.
